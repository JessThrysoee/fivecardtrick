How does it work?

The four face-up cards tell you the hidden one.

  1. Suit. The first face-up card has the hidden card's suit: ♦.
  2. Number. Rank the last three cards Low, Middle and High. Equal ranks go by suit, ♦ < ♥ < ♠ < ♣. Their order gives a number:
    LMH 1
    LHM 2
    MLH 3
    MHL 4
    HLM 5
    HML 6
    Codes starting with L are 1 or 2, M 3 or 4, H 5 or 6. If the last two cards go up, take the smaller number.
  3. Add. Count that many steps forward from the first face-up card. After K comes A.
