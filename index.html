How does it work?

The four face-up cards tell you the hidden one.

  1. Suit. The first card has the same suit as the hidden one: ♦.
  2. Number. Rank the last three cards Low, Middle and High. Equal ranks go by suit, ♦ < ♥ < ♠ < ♣. Their order gives a number:
    LMH 1
    LHM 2
    MLH 3
    MHL 4
    HLM 5
    HML 6
    Starting with L means 1 or 2, M means 3 or 4, H means 5 or 6. Take the smaller number when the last two cards go up.
  3. Add. Count forward from the first card. After K comes A.

♦5 + MHL = ♦5 + 4 = ♦9
♠J + 4 = ♠2 (J Q K A 2)
